LeashTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

LeashTracker ("we", "the app") is an app for households that share a dog, letting you track who has walked the dog. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, why, and what rights you have.

What data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and a hashed password.
  • Household data: household name, invite code, and which members belong to a household.
  • Dog data: the name(s) of the dog(s) in your household.
  • Walk data: the time of a walk and which household member logged it.
  • Notifications: a push notification token and your reminder preferences (days/times), so we can remind you if the dog hasn't been walked yet.
  • Subscription status: whether your household has a free or paid (Plus) subscription.

What we use this data for

Solely to provide the app's functionality: sharing walk status within your household, sending reminders, showing a leaderboard within your own household, and managing your subscription. We don't use your data for purposes outside the app, and we don't sell your data to third parties.

Third parties

We use the following external services:

  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the free version of the app. Ads are non-personalized by default; in the European Economic Area and the UK we ask for consent beforehand via a consent form.
  • RevenueCat — processes and manages subscriptions (Plus tier).
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — processes payments for subscriptions.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — used to deliver push notifications.

Where data is stored

Account data and walk data are stored on our own infrastructure. We have no commercial interest in retaining your data longer than needed for the app to function.

Retention period

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the email address below.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data, and the right to object to processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Children

LeashTracker is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children without parental or guardian consent.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date above reflects when the policy was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or your data? Email us at info@dyniq.nl.
